ENTRY 007 — PHASE 2B: VERTICAL MOVEMENT & GROUNDED JUMP PHYSICS

Date: 2026-11-10
Status: COMPLETED
Development phase: Phase 2B — Vertical Movement & Grounded Jump Physics

Objective:
Extend MovementController with functional vertical movement (GROUNDED/RISING/FALLING),
grounded jump physics with variable jump height, 100 ms jump buffer, 100 ms coyote time,
simple platform landing/collision, and test hazards. Extend MovementTestScene with visual
and telemetry support for all new features. No integration into campaign chapters this phase.

Physics Values Selected & Reasoning:
  jumpImpulse = 500 px/s
    Produces a full-hold apex of ~83 px (v²/2g ≈ 500²/(2×1500) ≈ 83 px), ≈1.5× character
    height. Short-hop (key released immediately, velocity ×0.5) gives ~21 px, enough to
    clear hazards (14 px tall). Values are snappy and readable at 60 fps.
  gravity = 1500 px/s²
    Air time ~333 ms for a full jump (2×500/1500). Avoids floaty feel. Compatible with 60
    fps and dt-based integration at typical 0.016 s increments.
  coyoteTime = 100 ms (0.10 s) — standard 6-frame grace period.
  jumpBuffer = 100 ms (0.10 s) — 6-frame look-ahead buffer.

Platform Model:
  Simple horizontal interval model: { x1, x2, y, label } — y is height above base ground
  in pixels. Landing is only triggered when FALLING (not RISING), preventing false landings
  when jumping upward through a platform. Tick scene resolves effective groundY from player
  pixel X each frame and passes it to updateVertical().

Hazard Model:
  Grounded hazard intervals: { x1, x2 } at base level. Damage triggered when player X is
  within range and verticalY <= 2 px (grounded or just landed). HAZARD_COOLDOWN = 1.0 s
  prevents frame-by-frame repeated damage hits. Hazard cleared by jumping over it.

Technical decisions:
  - False-landing prevention: Landing only allowed when state === FALLING. RISING players
    pass through platform groundY without triggering transition. This avoids a subtle bug
    where playerY(50) < platformY(90) would incorrectly snap the player down.
  - Jump-buffer on landing: Consumed immediately in the same frame the player touches ground,
    ensuring the buffered jump fires before any visual frame is drawn in the grounded state.
  - Coyote timer: Only starts when player leaves ground WITHOUT pressing jump (walk-off).
    Prevents extra coyote time after a deliberate jump.
  - Vertical and horizontal updates are independent: horizontal clamping/speed are unaffected
    by air state, matching the approved specification.
  - Variable jump height: Releasing Space while RISING multiplies verticalVelocity by 0.5,
    giving a ~21 px short hop vs ~83 px full jump (measured in automated tests).
